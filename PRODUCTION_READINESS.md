# Production Readiness Checklist

## Account lifecycle
- Sign-up, login, logout, deletion, and re-auth flows verified.
- Token refresh and expiry handling tested.

## Empty / offline / double-tap states
- Empty chat, no network, and rapid double-tap scenarios handled.

## Store review readiness
- Privacy policy, terms, and permission strings complete.
- Screenshots and metadata validated.

## Per-service billing inventory
- LLM provider, TTS, sync relay, and storage costs tracked.

## Hotfix procedures
- Rollback plan, canary promotion, and emergency patch process documented.
