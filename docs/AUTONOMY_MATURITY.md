# Autonomy Maturity

## L0-L3 Avatar Intelligence Roadmap

- L0: Manual assistance with full HITL.
- L1: Task-level autonomy with oversight metrics.
- L2: Rehearsal and bounded specialist creation.
- L3: Human-out-of-the-loop for reversible actions; HITL preserved for irreversible actions.

## Evidence
- OversightMetricsService tracks AOI.
- RehearsalMode logs outcomes.
- SpecialistSynthesisService tracks lineage and cap.

## Plan to L3
- Increase pre-approvals, improve rehearsal coverage, and maintain kill switch.
