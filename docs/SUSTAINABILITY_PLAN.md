# Shanaya Sustainability Plan

## Maintenance hours per module (estimated monthly)
- Client core: 20 hours
- Server/API: 15 hours
- Native mobile: 15 hours
- Desktop/Electron: 10 hours
- Docs/compliance: 10 hours
- Red-team/safety: 10 hours

## Bus-factor risks
- Several modules have a single maintainer.
- Provider adapters and native builds are high-risk.
- Documentation and compliance require ongoing ownership.

## Funding model
- GitHub Sponsors for individual contributors.
- Paid support for organizations.
- Commercial license option for hosted or branded deployments.
- Goal: fund at least one full-time maintainer if adoption grows.
